BODY EDUCATION

Facts, not myths.

General information to support understanding—not diagnose a condition. Seek professional care when something worries you.

01◌

Vulva & vagina

The vulva is the external genital area. The vagina is the internal muscular canal. They are not interchangeable terms. Normal external anatomy varies in shape, size and colour.

Read ACOG's guide ↗
02✧

Hymen & virginity myths

Virginity is not a medical diagnosis. The hymen's appearance cannot reliably prove sexual history; physical “virginity testing” has no valid medical basis. Never insert anything to test this.

Read ACOG's policy ↗
03♡

Everyday vulval care

Gently wash the external area; avoid perfumed intimate washes and do not douche inside the vagina. Discharge can be normal, but a new change with pain, itching or a strong smell deserves attention.

Read NHS guidance ↗
04⌁

Comfort & safer use

Body-safe, non-porous materials are easier to clean; sharing toys can pass infections. Water-based lubricant is a widely compatible starting point, but check toy and condom instructions.

Read Planned Parenthood's guide ↗
!

When to seek medical advice
Persistent pain, unusual bleeding, sores, a marked change in discharge or smell, fever, or other worrying changes should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional. This page cannot diagnose you.

A CLEAR PROMISE

More about
this space.

This ZIP is a static front-end prototype. It has useful local interactions but is not a live commerce or identity system.

01

Privacy & saved data

Saved concepts, local preferences and demo bag counts live in your browser storage. No login, backend or analytics is connected.

02

What is real here?

Search, filters, save/compare controls, prompt wheels, fit calculator and local manual image preview are front-end functions. Product images and descriptions are campaign concepts or references.

No real stock, supplier verification, checkout/payment, orders, user accounts, companion bookings, AI fitting or age verification.

03

Accessibility & choice

Keyboard-focus states, labelled controls, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support are included. Any adult prompt can be skipped; consent is never assumed.

04

Using the artwork

Image references are drawn from the supplied P2 repositories. This package uses remote raw GitHub image URLs for the art catalogue, so first-time image loading needs internet access.
